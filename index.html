BNI CHEAT SHEET

A quick-reference glossary for Referral Solutions members. Tap a term to see what it means.

1-2-1

One-to-One; a meeting between two BNI members to learn about each other and build the relationship needed to give better referrals.

Advanced MSP

Additional BNI member training courses beyond the foundational Member Success Program.

BNI Academy

BNI's online learning platform for member education and training.

BNI Connect

BNI's member platform/app for profiles, referrals, 1-2-1s, TYFCB and other member activity.

BNI

Business Network International

CEU

Chapter Education Unit; credit for BNI learning and education.

Contact Sphere

A broader group of complementary professions serving similar clients without directly competing.

DC

Director Consultant.

ED

Executive Director.

GAINS

Goals, Accomplishments, Interests, Networks & Skills; the framework used to learn more about another member during 1-2-1s.

Guest

A guest is an existing BNI member from another chapter, a director, or a non-business attendee. A visitor is a prospective member attending a chapter meeting. A sub is a member attending in place of another member, consistent with BNI attendance policies.

Givers Gain®

BNI's core philosophy: by helping others succeed and giving business first, opportunities are created through reciprocity.

LT

Leadership Team.

MC

Membership Committee.

MSP

Member Success Program; foundational BNI member training.

PALMS

The BNI member activity/reporting system used to track participation and activity.

Power Team

A group of members in complementary professions who can naturally refer business to one another.

Referral

A qualified business opportunity or introduction passed to another member; not simply a lead.

S/T

Secretary/Treasurer.

Sub

Substitute; someone who attends a meeting in place of a member when appropriate under BNI attendance policies.

TYFCB

Thank You for Closed Business; revenue generated from a BNI referral that resulted in closed business.

VCP

Visibility, Credibility, Profitability; the relationship-building process from being known, to being trusted, to generating business.

Visitor

A visitor is anyone attending a BNI chapter meeting who is not currently a member. A guest is a visitor invited by a member; a sub attends in place of a member when appropriate under BNI attendance policies.

VP

Vice President.
